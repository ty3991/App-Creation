## Product and geometry review

Retained the existing stack and versioned generator. Tests cover sign/tray/plate manifold meshes, millimeter bounds, independent STL decoding, plate hole centers, degenerate faces and oriented edges, printer-volume review, immutable snapshots, owner revisions/approval, attachment ownership and payload-bound idempotency. Existing local customer records and API credentials were preserved. Geometry source/version was not changed. Mesh checks establish the tested geometric properties, not mechanical strength, fit or universal self-intersection detection for arbitrary third-party CAD.

Pricing remains application arithmetic from owner inputs, followed by owner-controlled final price and approval. No automated quote sending, payment or printing was added. Uploaded production CAD remains inert private data; it is not executed or promised to be a validated parametric design. Owner inspection in Bambu Studio remains required.

## Limits requiring environment or a product decision

No public host or GoDaddy editor changes, merge, DNS changes, subscription purchases, unofficial ChatGPT API automation, local-model install, or automatic deletion were performed. Public staging, backups outside this PC, operational monitoring, physical phone accessibility/interaction, broad assistive-technology testing and an actual printed-part fit check remain unverified. Malware scanning and automatic owner recovery would require additional operational design; uploads are restricted, private, sandboxed downloads and never executed. Use a limited reviewed launch after the listed gates, not a claim of unconditional production readiness.
